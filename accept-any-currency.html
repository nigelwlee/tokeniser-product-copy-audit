Accept any currency

Accept any currency, settle in your own.

Accept subscriptions in fiat currencies, stablecoins and other approved digital assets. Every payment is converted and settles in your fund's denomination currency.

Get the briefs Book a Demo

The problem today

Currency conversion on every transaction

Most funds accept only their denomination currency, so offshore and digital asset investors must convert before they invest. A fund with many New Zealand investors, for example, pays for NZD to AUD conversion on every subscription and distribution.

What changes for your fund

Access to international capital.

Enable investors to subscribe in the currency they already hold, wherever they are based. This supports global and USD-denominated funds, including those domiciled in Singapore or the British Virgin Islands.

Reach digital asset holders.

A significant pool of stablecoin capital is seeking quality assets. Enable investors who hold stablecoins to subscribe directly, while your fund receives its denomination currency.

Simpler currency operations.

Foreign exchange is built into the transaction, so your team does not manage separate conversions. Every subscription is recorded on your register in your fund's denomination currency.

How it works

Converted and settled,
in a single transaction

An investor pays in their chosen currency, and our settlement partner converts it into a digital Australian dollar on Redbelly. The investor does not need to move funds between networks, and the subscription settles to your register in one step, or not at all.

Get ready now

Prepare now, activate when ready

  • Onboard your fund and investors now, alongside your current registry.
  • You choose which currencies and digital assets your fund accepts.
  • Speak with our team about the currencies relevant to your investors.

"How it works" diagram brief

Today: Investor holds NZD or USDT → converts through a bank or exchange → pays AUD to the fund. On Tokeniser: Investor pays in NZD, USD or USDT → converted to digital AUD → settled on your register. Tagline: "Settled in your currency."

Holds applied

  • The settlement partner is not named ("our settlement partner").
  • No claims about speed or live availability: no "instant", "minutes", "T+0" or "available now". "In one step, or not at all" describes how the transaction completes, not how fast.
  • Written for fund managers, without web3 jargon. "Stablecoin" is used only where investors already hold crypto.
  • Follows the audit rules: issuer lens, no contractions, 2 sentences max, no AI tells, institutional tone.
