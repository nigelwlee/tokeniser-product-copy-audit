Website copy audit · Fix #1 · Product & Superpowers

Speak to the issuer,
not the investor.

Lines on the Product and Superpowers pages slip into the investor's voice, as in the Collateral Mobility hero "Your holdings, no longer stuck." Our reader is the service provider / asset issuer. Below is every line that changes, with the live copy, the proposed copy, and screenshots of both rendered on the live page.

Audited against QA preview PR-159 on . The "After" screenshots were made by swapping the proposed copy into the live page, so they show real fonts and line breaks.

"You" is the issuer."You/your" always means the fund, company or service provider. It never means the investor.
Issuer outcome first.Headlines name what the issuer gets: capital retained, AUM grown, cost cut, edge won.
Investors are offered things.Investor benefits are framed as what the issuer gives: "Let investors…", "Give investors…".
Voice unchanged.Short headline plus italic second line, Australian spelling, same claims and numbers.

Summary
